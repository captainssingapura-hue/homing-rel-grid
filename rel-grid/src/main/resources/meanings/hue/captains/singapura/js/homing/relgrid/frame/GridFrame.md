# The grid's frame

## GridFrame

Everything a grid of cells shows the user beyond its cells: where it ends, what parts one cell from the next, the bands its columns and rows are named in, where the user is and what they have chosen, and what it lays over its cells while it edits or waits.

## GridEdge

Where the grid ends and what it stands on, and the sign that the keys are with it.

## Lattice

The lines that part one cell from the next, along a row and down a column.

## CursorBorder

The place the grid would go on from, while the keys are elsewhere.

## HeldCursorBorder

The place the keys act on, while the grid holds them.

## LentCursorBorder

The place whose cell has taken the keys to work in it.

## SelectionFill

The cells the user has chosen to act on together.

## HeaderBand

The band the columns are named in.

## GutterBand

The band the rows are numbered in, and the numbers.

## EditorAnchor

Where a cell's editor opens, over the cell it edits.

## Mask

What tells the user the grid waits on an answer, and is not to be worked meanwhile.

## QuestionPanel

Where the grid puts a question to the user while it waits.

## MergeHost

One cell that reaches across several columns of its row.

## ResizeGuide

Where a column's edge will land while the user resizes it.

## CopiedArea

The cells the grid last copied, shown as copied - until Escape, an edit, the next copy or a rearranged view.

## CopiedBorder

The line round the cells the grid last copied - the marching ants of a spreadsheet, or whatever a design draws there.
